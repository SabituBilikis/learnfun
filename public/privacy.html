Learn Fun Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Learn Fun is an educational app for children aged 1–5. We designed it so children can learn without creating an account or sharing personal information.

Information we collect

Learn Fun does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not use advertising, third-party analytics, location tracking, camera access, microphone access, or child accounts.

On-device learning progress

Child profile names, learning progress, rewards, sound preferences, screen-time settings, and a protected Parent PIN verifier are stored only on the device. Removing the app or clearing its app data removes this information. We cannot access or restore it.

Public website measurement

Optional measurement on our public homepage, learning guides, and feature pages is separate from the learning app. Collection is disabled unless enabled for the production website. When enabled, we send page visits and clicks on Play Online and Google Play links to Plausible Analytics. The learning app, lessons, games, and parent settings are excluded.

Events contain approved page paths, page types, fixed button locations, and broad referral categories. We do not send child names, ages, PINs, learning progress, account identifiers, arbitrary URL parameters, or full referring URLs. We do not use advertising pixels, session recordings, analytics cookies, or persistent browser identifiers. Measurement respects the browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.

Plausible receives the IP address and browser information needed to handle a request. Its policy describes using these to derive daily identifiers and approximate location without storing raw IP addresses or raw user-agent strings. Its dashboard presents aggregate statistics; the provider processes event and session records. See Plausible's data policy.

Google Play links on public pages may include fixed campaign labels identifying the source page. They contain no visitor or child identifiers. Following an external store link is subject to Google's privacy practices.

Parents and guardians

Parents can manage profiles and screen-time settings inside the app. The parent area is protected by a six-digit Parent PIN, with a temporary lockout after repeated failed attempts.

Children's privacy

Because Learn Fun is intended for young children, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before that version is released.
