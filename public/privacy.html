Learn Fun Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Learn Fun is an educational app for children aged 1–5. We designed it so children can learn without creating an account or sharing personal information.

Information we collect

Learn Fun does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not use advertising, third-party analytics, location tracking, camera access, microphone access, or child accounts.

On-device learning progress

Child profile names, learning progress, rewards, sound preferences, screen-time settings, and a protected Parent PIN verifier are stored only on the device. Removing the app or clearing its app data removes this information. We cannot access or restore it.

Parents and guardians

Parents can manage profiles and screen-time settings inside the app. The parent area is protected by a six-digit Parent PIN, with a temporary lockout after repeated failed attempts.

Children's privacy

Because Learn Fun is intended for young children, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before that version is released.
